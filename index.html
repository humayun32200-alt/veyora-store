✦ Shipping & Delivery ⌄

At VEYORA Store, we aim to deliver orders safely and promptly. Orders usually arrive within 3–7 business days, although timing may vary by location, courier service, and other circumstances. Please ensure your shipping address and contact number are correct. Where tracking is available, use the provided details to check progress. Delivery charges, if applicable, are shown on the product page or during checkout.

✦ Returns & Refunds ⌄

Where the courier service allows it, check your parcel in front of the courier before accepting delivery. If you notice visible damage, missing items, or an incorrect product, inform the courier immediately and contact our support team as soon as possible. Return or refund requests are reviewed according to the circumstances and our applicable policy. Please contact us before sending any item back.

✦ Privacy Policy ⌄

VEYORA Store respects your privacy. We collect necessary information, such as your name, phone number, and delivery address, to process and deliver orders and provide customer support. Where necessary, relevant information may be shared with delivery partners and payment service providers to fulfil your order. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access or misuse.

✦ Terms of Service ⌄

By using the VEYORA Store website, you agree to comply with our terms and conditions. Orders are subject to product availability and confirmation. Prices, descriptions, availability, and store policies may be updated when necessary. Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact and delivery details. VEYORA Store may review or cancel orders where necessary, subject to applicable law. Relevant return, refund, and delivery conditions are communicated through store information.
